This policy applies to all domestic and international business travel.

## Approval Requirements

Business travel requires manager approval at least 10 working days in advance.
International travel additionally requires department head approval.

## Flight Class

Economy class is standard for all flights under 6 hours. Business class may be
approved for flights exceeding 6 hours with department head authorization.

## Accommodation Limits

Hotel accommodation is capped at 800 SAR per night domestically and 1200 SAR
per night internationally. Exceptions require prior written approval.

## Daily Allowance

A daily allowance of 250 SAR covers meals and incidental expenses. Receipts are
not required for the daily allowance.
