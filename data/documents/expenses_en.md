This policy governs all employee expense claims.

## Submission Deadline

Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense date. Claims
submitted after this period will not be reimbursed.

## Receipt Requirements

Receipts are required for any single expense above 100 SAR. Claims without a
valid receipt above this threshold will be rejected.

## Approved Expense Categories

Reimbursable categories include business travel, client meals, professional
training, and work-related software. Personal entertainment is not covered.

## Reimbursement Timeline

Approved claims are paid with the next monthly payroll cycle, provided approval
was granted at least 5 working days before payroll processing.
