All employees must comply with this policy. Violations may result in
disciplinary action.

## Password Requirements

Passwords must be at least 12 characters and include letters, numbers, and
symbols. Passwords must be rotated every 90 days and may not be reused.

## Two-Factor Authentication

Two-factor authentication is mandatory on all company accounts. Sharing
passwords or authentication codes with any person is strictly prohibited.

## Device Encryption

All company laptops and mobile devices must use full disk encryption. Personal
devices may not be used to store company data.

## Reporting Lost Devices

A lost or stolen device must be reported to the IT service desk within 2 hours
of discovery, regardless of the time of day.
