This policy applies to all full-time employees of Falcon Logistics Group.

## Annual Leave Entitlement

Employees are entitled to 21 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
Employees with more than five years of continuous service receive 25 days.

## Carrying Over Unused Leave

Unused annual leave may be carried over for up to 6 months into the following
year. Any balance remaining after this period expires and is not compensated.

## Requesting Leave

Leave requests must be submitted at least 14 days in advance through the HR
portal and require approval from the direct manager. Requests covering more
than 10 consecutive days require additional approval from the department head.

## Leave During Probation

Employees serving their probation period may not take annual leave except in
documented emergencies approved by HR.
